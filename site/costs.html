Staffing

The authoritative staffing answer is a ratio, and almost no operator publishes one. Where a facility publishes both staff and beds we show both columns above so the ratio can be read directly. For planning, the defensible published benchmarks are 1.0–2.5 direct-care FTE per psychiatric bed depending on acuity; anything in that range is a planning assumption, not a measurement, and this project does not store it as data.

Comparison: harm versus spend

Overdose deaths and homeless counts sit side by side on state pages; the overdose series is in metric (7,592 rows). Neither dataset attributes a death to homelessness, and we do not compute a ratio that implies it.
