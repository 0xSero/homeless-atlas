# la-brief — Los Angeles: the biggest unsheltered population in the country
Write reports/los-angeles.md in the same shape as reports/san-francisco.md, generated by a script
(scripts/la_report.py) that pulls every number from the database with an inline citation:
PIT history 2007-2025 for CA-600 and CA-601..607, unsheltered share, chronic counts; federal awards by
program and recipient; the top 30 Los Angeles County organisations by 990 revenue and the people running
them; LAHSA and County budget lines where present; closed/open psychiatric facilities and jails
(Twin Towers, Men's Central Jail, LACUSC, Olive View); Measure H / Prop HHH; the LA Alliance settlement;
overdose deaths; and a cost model with † on every derived figure.
accept: >=100 cited links, zero uncited numeric claims, sections that cannot be computed say so
        explicitly, rendered at /report/los-angeles.html.
